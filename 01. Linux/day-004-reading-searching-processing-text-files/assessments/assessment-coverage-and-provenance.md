# Assessment completeness and provenance

- **Final knowledge assessment:** The five multiple-choice questions in `final-assessment.md` are the actual final assessment completed in the classroom; final answers B, C, D, D, B (5/5 correct).
- **Intermediate classroom knowledge checks:** The lesson had checks during foundational stages and project substeps. Exact wording for every historical question was not recoverable from the available material. `knowledge-check.md` therefore contains **reconstructed review questions**, not falsely attributed verbatim transcripts.
- **Practical work:** Follow-along commands, incident log contents, seven-section report structure, SHA-256 validation and final file tree were documented from verified learning progress.
- **Screenshots:** No historical screenshots are included because source image files were not available to package. Students are instructed to capture their own.
- **Practical assessment:** `practical-assessment.md` is an independent learner assessment, separate from the five-question final knowledge assessment.

This distinction prevents educational reconstruction from being misrepresented as a verbatim historical record.
